PlaneTxt is an opinionated, future-proof app to manage your life in plain text: todo, agenda, journal and notes.

Everything is stored as Markdown files, in a folder you choose or in the app's private storage. One daily file per day holds your journal, todos and events; notes live in subfolders. There is no database and no lock-in: sync the folder with Syncthing, Dropbox or anything else, and edit the files with any text editor.

Features:

- Month calendar with the Markdown content of the selected day below it, swipe between days
- Check and uncheck todos with a tap
- Each morning, carry over the todos left open on previous days, moved or copied
- Week review: todos done, still open or carried over, and the week's events
- Search across all days and notes, accents ignored
- Notes in folders, with search, rename, sharing and archiving
- Events imported from a shared calendar file (.ics), shared text saved as a note
- Home screen widget showing today's agenda and todos
- Reminders through local notifications, with an adjustable delay
- Safe with sync tools: changes made elsewhere are detected before saving, and conflict copies are reported
- Light and dark themes
- No account, no tracking, no network access

File layout:

- dailies/20250128.md
- archives/
- notes/Work/project.md
